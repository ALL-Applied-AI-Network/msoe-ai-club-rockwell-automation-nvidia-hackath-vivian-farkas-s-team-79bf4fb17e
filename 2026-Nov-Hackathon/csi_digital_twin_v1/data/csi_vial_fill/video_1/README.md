# Video 1 vial-fill data

This folder contains the vial-fill Video 1 source extracted from the supplied
dataset ZIP. The video is 640 × 480 at 30 fps and the dataset documents 19
vials. No recipe labels are provided for this video.

The exploratory comparison against Video 5's recipe references is in
`../unlabeled_video_analysis/RECIPE_CANDIDATE_ASSESSMENT.md`. It does not assign
confirmed recipe IDs. Regenerate Videos 1-4 together from the project root with
`python tools/analyze_unlabeled_video_recipes.py`.
